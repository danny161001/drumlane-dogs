Drumlane Dogs

Stop booking dogs whose vaccinations have expired, without chasing paper records.

Typical problems we heard (invented for this prototype)

"I'm bathing a dog with wet hands and can't answer WhatsApp, so people ring again and book elsewhere."

"I never know if a dog's vaccinations are in date until they're already on the table."

How it works
  1. Step 1

    Capacity limits

    Bookings are limited to the places you set and a full day offers the nearest day with room.

  2. Step 2

    Automated vaccination status

    A dog's vaccination is checked, amber when expiring within 14 days, red and blocked when expired.

  3. Step 3

    One-tap notes & owner view

    One-tap notes, and an owner view that shows only their own dog.

Honest note

The demo is a daycare version built as a prototype, with sample data only.

Appointment time slots, grooming-specific notes and reminders are Planned, not built.
